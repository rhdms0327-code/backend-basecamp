```markdown
| MM/DD | [문제명](https://school.programmers.co.kr/learn/courses/30/lessons/번호) | 유형 | [P번호](https://github.com/rhdms0327-code/programmers-csharp/blob/main/src/Solutions/폴더/P번호.cs) | 핵심 한 줄 |
```

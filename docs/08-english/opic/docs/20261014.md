**1. 내 답변** (빨간 곳이 틀린 부분)

Excuse me, I just <mark>had the message</mark>① from the airline that my flight is canceled and I just want to know <mark>how I have to do next</mark>②. <mark>Can I take my money back</mark>③ from this canceled flight? Or <mark>is there other airplane flights next few hours</mark>④? <mark>Can I take that seat</mark>⑤, or can you give me any other things to solve this problem? Because I need to go back to my country, I don't have any money right now, and I don't have a place to sleep tonight.

| 번호 | 틀린 것 | 고친 것 | 이유 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ① | had the message | got a message | get a message가 자연스러움 |
| ② | how I have to do next | what I should do next | what + should |
| ③ | Can I take my money back | Can I get a full refund | refund 사용 |
| ④ | is there other airplane flights next few hours | Is there another flight in the next few hours | another + 단수, in the next |
| ⑤ | Can I take that seat | Can you put me on that flight | 항공편에 태워 달라는 표현 |

**2. 교정본 전체** (쉐도잉, `/`에서 끊어 읽기)

> Excuse me, / I just got a message that my flight is canceled, / and I really need to know what I should do next. / Can you get me on another flight / leaving in the next few hours, / or can I get a full refund? / I really need to get back to my country, / and I don't have any money or a place to sleep tonight, / so I desperately need to figure this out today.

**3. 이렇게도 말해요**

- **Can I get a full refund?** → I'd like a full refund, please. · Could I get my money back?
- **another flight** → the next flight out · a flight later today

**4. 이 주제에서 쓰기 좋은 표현**

| 표현 | 예문 | 뜻 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Could you help me ~? | Could you help me rebook my flight? | 항공편을 다시 예약하도록 도와주실 수 있나요? |
| Is there another ~? | Is there another flight leaving today? | 오늘 출발하는 다른 항공편이 있나요? |
| I'd like a full refund. | If there's no flight today, I'd like a full refund. | 오늘 항공편이 없으면 전액 환불을 받고 싶어요. |

### Q9 · 돌발: 여행 중 길 찾기, 기술의 변화

**질문**: Tell me about a time when you had a similar problem while traveling, and how technology has changed navigation.

**1. 내 답변** (빨간 곳이 틀린 부분)

While traveling in New York, I <mark>had trouble looking at the map and finding directions</mark>①. I didn't know the way, so every single block I had to stop people and ask if I was going the right direction. But recently, navigation apps and GPS are amazing — the map automatically rotates to show you exactly where to head. <mark>Ten years ago, digital maps didn't work nearly as well.</mark>②

| 번호 | 틀린 것 | 고친 것 | 이유 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ① | had trouble looking at the map and finding directions | had a hard time reading physical maps | 지도는 read |
| ② | Ten years ago, digital maps didn't work nearly as well. | It's so much easier to travel now compared to ten years ago! | 비교는 현재를 기준으로 끝맺기 |

**2. 교정본 전체** (쉐도잉, `/`에서 끊어 읽기)

> On that same trip to New York, / I had such a hard time reading physical maps. / I was constantly getting lost, / so I literally had to stop people on every single block / to ask if I was going the right way. / But these days, map apps and GPS are just amazing. / The map automatically rotates to show exactly which way to go, / tracking your live location. / It's so much easier to travel now / compared to ten years ago!

**3. 이렇게도 말해요**

- **I got lost.** → I kept getting lost. · I had no idea where I was.
- **navigation apps** → map apps · GPS apps like Google Maps

**4. 이 주제에서 쓰기 좋은 표현**

| 표현 | 예문 | 뜻 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I kept getting lost. | I kept getting lost, so I had to ask for directions a lot. | 계속 길을 잃어서 자주 길을 물어봐야 했어요. |
| These days, ~ | These days, I just pull up a map app on my phone. | 요즘은 휴대폰으로 지도 앱을 열면 돼요. |
| It makes ~ so much easier. | It makes traveling so much easier. | 여행이 훨씬 쉬워졌어요. |

### Q10 · 재택근무 vs 사무실 출근

**질문**: What do you think about working from home versus working in the office?

**1. 내 답변** (빨간 곳이 틀린 부분)

To be honest, <mark>every worker loves to work at home</mark>①. But <mark>from the mindset of a business manager or a boss controlling workers</mark>②, I wouldn't prefer people working at home. <mark>Honestly, who creates the best outputs working from home versus working at the office?</mark>③ People need face-to-face communication. Google Meet allows communication, but it is totally different from sharing the same office space and interacting in person. The synergy and outputs are much stronger in the office.

| 번호 | 틀린 것 | 고친 것 | 이유 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ① | every worker loves to work at home | everyone loves working from home | work at home이 아니라 work from home |
| ② | from the mindset of a business manager or a boss controlling workers | from a manager's point of view | 관리자 관점은 point of view |
| ③ | Honestly, who creates the best outputs ...? | (삭제) | 질문을 던진 뒤 답이 없어 흐름이 끊김 |

**2. 교정본 전체** (쉐도잉, `/`에서 끊어 읽기)

> You know, from a worker's perspective, / everyone loves working from home. / But if I think about it from a manager's point of view, / office work is definitely better. / Sure, you can use Google Meet to communicate, / but it's just not the same as being in the same room. / When people are actually working together face-to-face / in the same place, / the communication, synergy, and overall output / are just on a completely different level.

**3. 이렇게도 말해요**

- **everyone loves working from home** → most people prefer working from home · people enjoy the flexibility of working from home
- **definitely better** → a better fit · more effective for us

**4. 이 주제에서 쓰기 좋은 표현**

| 표현 | 예문 | 뜻 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| From my perspective, ~ | From my perspective, working in the office is more effective. | 제 관점에서는 사무실 근무가 더 효율적이에요. |
| On the one hand, ~ On the other hand, ~ | On the one hand, it saves commuting time. On the other hand, communication takes longer. | 한편으로는 통근 시간을 아끼지만, 다른 한편으로는 소통이 느려져요. |
| It really depends on ~ | It really depends on the type of work. | 일의 종류에 따라 달라요. |

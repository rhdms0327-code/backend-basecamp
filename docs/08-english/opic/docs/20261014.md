IM2에서 IH로 올라가려면 아래 세 가지가 가장 큰 차이로 보입니다. 아래 답변 10개를 기준으로 한 분석입니다.

| 영역 | 현재 모습 | IH를 위한 목표 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 문장 구성 | 짧은 문장을 `So`, `you know`로 이어 붙이고 중간에 문장이 끊김 | 한 문장 안에서 이유와 결과를 연결어로 이어 말하기 |
| 문단 구성 | 과거 경험(뉴욕 여행)은 구조가 좋으나, 묘사와 루틴은 나열에 그침 | 모든 유형에서 "도입 → 구체 예시 → 느낀 점" 마무리 |
| 정확도 | 관사, 의문문 어순, 시제에서 반복 오류 | 반복 오류 3~4가지를 집중 교정 |

## 내 답변과 교정본

### 1. 거주지 날씨

- **내 답변**: In our city, in [City], the weather is quite cold at night and early morning, but in the lunchtime it is warm. So currently people are wearing, you know, short t-shirts and wear some cardigans to warm up at afternoons.
- **교정본**: Here in [City], it gets quite chilly at night and early in the morning, but it warms right up around lunchtime. Because of this big temperature gap, you'll see people wearing short sleeves with a light cardigan, you know, so they can easily stay warm or cool down depending on the time of day.

### 2. 자기소개 및 직업

- **내 답변**: I am working at the IT company, it's called [Company]. And at [Company] I am the developer, I'm back-end developer. So what is back-end developer does is, so when you see the website, there are some something going on at the background system. And I'm building that. And what is the background system is, so to run the website, there are something, you know, database, and like showing how to showing, there are some background things to do. So that is what I'm building.
- **교정본**: I work as a back-end developer at an IT company called [Company]. You know when you look at a website, there are all these hidden systems running in the background, like databases? Well, my job is to build and manage those systems to make sure the website runs smoothly without any issues.

### 3. 롤플레이: 상대의 거주지 질문하기

- **내 답변**: What country are you live in right now, and where you're from? And which city are you living in? And I want to know about are you living in city or, you know, sideways or countries? And how is it looks like? Do you have any mountains around you, or how's the weather is like? There are many rains at your city where you're living in? And is it always warm, or is it have, you know, four seasons: spring, summer, winters? And I want to know how is it, you know, houses looks like. Are you living in an apartment or just house?
- **교정본**: I'm really curious, what country and city do you live in right now? And do you live right in the middle of the city, or more out in the suburbs? Also, what's the weather like there? Do you get a lot of rain, or do you have four distinct seasons like we do here? Oh, and one more thing—do you live in an apartment or a house?

### 4. 가족과의 일상, 출산 후 변화

- **내 답변**: My daily routine, I wake up early morning around 6:30 and I take a shower quickly. Right after that, I put on some clothes and prepare for head out to the office. When I'm ready, after that, I prepare my child for the kindergarten. I take a bag, some stuffs, water bottles for my baby, set up the bag. And after all is ready, I take out the clothes from the closet and put the clothes to the baby, the pants, and change the diaper and put some socks while she is sleeping. Recently, I just gave up preparing breakfast for the baby before she goes to the kindergarten because she doesn't feel like eating breakfast very early in the morning. She goes to the kindergarten around 7:30. Both my husband and I need to go to the office early. So I just drop her off before 7:30, say goodbye, and go to the office quickly.
- **교정본**: My mornings are super busy these days. I wake up around 6:30, quickly grab a shower, and get dressed for work. Then, I pack my baby's daycare bag with her water bottle and stuff. To save time, I usually change her diaper and get her dressed while she's still half asleep. Since both my husband and I have to be at the office pretty early, we drop her off at daycare before 7:30, say a quick goodbye, and rush to work.

### 5. 좋아하는 영화와 기억에 남는 관람

- **내 답변**: Just any movie that people all love. For example, when ten million people are watching a movie, that means it is a great movie. Any movie that is famous and people are talking about it, I watch the movie to get in the conversation. What was memorable about the movie 'Odyssey' is that after my baby was born, I had no time to watch movies and go to the theater. But recently it was a big hit, so I asked my mom to take care of my baby for a few hours. My husband and I went to the theater and watched it. It was a very relaxing time and it had been a while since we went to the theater.
- **교정본**: I honestly just love watching big box-office hits that everyone is talking about, mostly so I can easily join the conversation with my friends. The most memorable time recently was when I watched a massive hit movie. Since my baby was born, I basically had no time for the theater. But my mom generously offered to babysit for a few hours, so my husband and I finally got to go. It was such a relaxing and well-deserved break for us.

### 6. 자주 가는 공원

- **내 답변**: Recently I go to as many parks as I can because I want to give many experiences to my baby, especially nature. My baby likes to pick up some sticks and rocks, runs through the field and grass, and looks at bugs. I really love spending time with my baby running through nature, seeing trees, mountains, and animals like dogs at the park. So I really enjoyed the time at the park.
- **교정본**: Lately, I try to take my baby to the park as much as possible so she can really experience nature. She just loves running around on the grass, picking up little rocks and sticks, and looking at bugs or dogs passing by. I absolutely love spending that time with her outdoors—it's just great bonding time for us.

### 7. 가장 기억에 남는 여행 (뉴욕)

- **내 답변**: When I was in college, in my third year, I won a scholarship because my grades were good. The college gave me scholarship money for one year. My parents were proud of me and supported me to go on a trip far away. My mom contacted a friend living in New York and asked if I could stay for one or two months, and she said yes. In New York, I tried to save as much money as I could. I bought a 30-centimeter Subway sandwich, cut it into small pieces, and ate it across breakfast, lunch, and dinner. It was winter, very cold, and I had a limited budget. When it was freezing, I went into Starbucks, bought a cheap drink, and warmed up before walking again. What makes a trip memorable is enduring those tough times.
- **교정본**: When I was a junior in college, I got a scholarship, and my parents were so proud that they supported a solo trip to New York for me. My mom actually asked her friend in NY to let me stay there. Since I was on a super tight budget, I had to be really frugal. I'd buy one footlong Subway sandwich and split it for breakfast, lunch, and dinner! It was a freezing winter, so whenever I got too cold, I'd pop into a Starbucks, buy the cheapest drink to warm up, and then keep walking. Honestly, pushing through those hard times is what made the trip so unforgettable.

### 8. 롤플레이: 항공편 취소

- **내 답변**: Excuse me, I just had the message from the airline that my flight is canceled and I just want to know how I have to do next. Can I take my money back from this canceled flight? Or is there other airplane flights next few hours? Can I take that seat, or can you give me any other things to solve this problem? Because I need to go back to my country, I don't have any money right now, and I don't have a place to sleep tonight.
- **교정본**: Excuse me, I just got a message that my flight is canceled, and I really need to know what I should do next. Can you get me on another flight leaving in the next few hours, or can I get a full refund? I really need to get back to my country, and I don't have any money or a place to sleep tonight, so I desperately need to figure this out today.

### 9. 돌발: 여행 중 길 찾기, 기술의 변화

- **내 답변**: While traveling in New York, I had trouble looking at the map and finding directions. I didn't know the way, so every single block I had to stop people and ask if I was going the right direction. But recently, navigation apps and GPS are amazing—the map automatically rotates to show you exactly where to head. Ten years ago, digital maps didn't work nearly as well.
- **교정본**: On that same trip to New York, I had such a hard time reading physical maps. I was constantly getting lost, so I literally had to stop people on every single block to ask if I was going the right way. But these days, map apps and GPS are just amazing. The map automatically rotates to show exactly which way to go, tracking your live location. It's so much easier to travel now compared to ten years ago!

### 10. 재택근무 vs 사무실 출근

- **내 답변**: To be honest, every worker loves to work at home. But from the mindset of a business manager or a boss controlling workers, I wouldn't prefer people working at home. Honestly, who creates the best outputs working from home versus working at the office? People need face-to-face communication. Google Meet allows communication, but it is totally different from sharing the same office space and interacting in person. The synergy and outputs are much stronger in the office.
- **교정본**: You know, from a worker's perspective, everyone loves working from home. But if I think about it from a manager's point of view, office work is definitely better. Sure, you can use Google Meet to communicate, but it's just not the same as being in the same room. When people are actually working together face-to-face in the same place, the communication, synergy, and overall output are just on a completely different level.

## 수정사항

| 번호 | 내 답변의 문제 | 수정 방향 | 예시 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | 설명이 길고 같은 말이 반복됨 (2번) | 한 문장 정의 + 비유 한 가지로 끝내기 | "hidden systems running in the background, like databases" |
| 2 | 의문문 어순과 전치사 (3번) | 질문 앞에 "do you live in"으로 시작 | "What country are you live in" → "What country do you live in?" |
| 3 | 첫 문장이 평이하고 길어짐 (1, 2번) | "Here in ~", "I work as ~"처럼 주어와 동사를 먼저 | "In our city, in [City], the weather is..." → "Here in [City], it gets quite chilly..." |
| 4 | 관사, 수 일치 오류 | 직업은 "a back-end developer", 단수·복수 확인 | "I'm back-end developer" → "I work as a back-end developer" |
| 5 | 영화 취향이 막연함 (5번) | 장르나 이유를 한 가지 구체적으로 | "big box-office hits that everyone is talking about" |
| 6 | 마무리 문장이 약함 (6번) | 느낀 점을 한 문장으로 | "great bonding time for us" |
| 7 | 롤플레이 질문이 길고 중복됨 (3번) | 질문 3~4개로 끊기, 질문마다 한 문장 | 도시 → 환경(도심/교외) → 날씨 → 집 형태 순서 |
| 8 | 대안 제시가 흐릿함 (8번) | "또는" 두 가지를 분명히 말하기 | "another flight ... or a full refund" |

### 잘한 점

- **7번(뉴욕 여행)**: 서브웨이 30cm, 스타벅스 같은 구체적 디테일과 "힘든 시간을 견딘 것이 기억에 남게 한다"는 마무리가 있어 상위 등급에 필요한 스토리 구성이 갖춰졌습니다.
- **9번(길 찾기)**: 과거 경험에서 현재 기술 변화로 이어지는 비교 구조가 자연스럽습니다.
- **10번(재택근무)**: 근로자와 관리자 두 관점을 나눠 말해 이슈 문제의 틀이 잡혔습니다.
